ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Supporting Industry
Through Reliable Solutions.

Thai Tank Terminal Limited (TTT) is an independent liquid terminal operator located in the Map Ta Phut area of Rayong, Thailand.

The company provides storage and handling services for petrochemical and petroleum products, supporting industrial customers through terminal operations and product transportation.

TTT is a joint venture involving PTT Global Chemical Public Company Limited and Vopak.
